**Title**: Testing the network centrality hypothesis within process-based acceptance and commitment therapy – A single case experiment utilizing perceived causal networks

Network analysis needs intensive longitudinal data. One can use baseline data to achieve that. One can also use [[PECAN]]: self-reports about how other variables influence the others. 

The idea is that [[PBT]] can also be delivered with a subset of processes, just as [[PECAN]] can be used with a subset of variables. Than they have as goal the increment of [[psychological flexibility]] through openness, awareness and engagement.
There is tho no consensus on which to start with first! One should use an idiographic network approach to decide which process is most important for the client.

The idea here is to look after if treatment calibrated through the most central node assessed through a [[PECAN]] survey is more effective than the least central node.

The [[ACT]] protocol is not [[chronic pain]] specific.

They used the [online PECAN tool](https://pecan-tool.rpsychologist.com/) to show the results.
